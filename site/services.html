Services

One crew.Both seasons.

Noah runs two businesses out of one truck. Use the switch to see the half of the year you need.

April — October

Green season.

Weekly maintenance, one-off rescues and the overgrown jobs other companies won't quote.

01

Mowing

Weekly or biweekly, cut to leave a clean stripe. Residential lawns through to commercial lots and acreage.

02

Trimming & edging

String trimming around beds, fences and foundations. Crisp edges where lawn meets bed or drive.

03

Mulch & stone

Bark mulch, river stone and weed barrier. Beds dug, shaped and topped by the yard.

04

Spring & fall cleanups

Leaf and debris clearing, bed cutbacks, and getting a property ready for the season either side.

05

Overgrown reclamation

Brush hogging and heavy clearing for lots that have got away. The jobs that get declined elsewhere.

06

Beds & light hardscape

New garden beds, low retaining walls, weed barrier and fill. Planting and shaping to match the house.

November — March

White season.

Cleared early enough to get out of the driveway. Seasonal contracts and one-off storm calls.

01

Driveway plowing

Residential drives and commercial lots, cleared before the working day starts.

02

Hand shovelling

Walkways, steps and entries done by hand where a plow can't reach.

03

Sanding & ice

Sand and treatment on the slopes and shaded stretches that ice over first.

04

Storm calls

One-off plows after a storm, including short notice. Several reviews start exactly here.

05

Entry clearing

Digging out doorways, mailboxes and parking so the property stays usable mid-storm.

06

Season contracts

Booked for the whole winter, so you're on the route before the first flake.
